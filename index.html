My Portfolio

  1. The Poultry Farm Intelligence
    • An AI sales analyst for a poultry business that runs every evening at 7 PM. It pulls the day’s orders from Google Sheets and calculates revenue, units sold, top products, top sales channels and peak sales hours. GPT-4o then turns those numbers into a business report covering finances, best sellers, hidden problems, growth opportunities and next steps. The report goes to the owner by Gmail. It is built to use only the sales data, so the AI doesn’t invent figures.

      Stack: n8n, Google Sheets, JavaScript, OpenAI GPT-4o, Gmail



  2. Lead Routing & Sales Process
    • A lead-intake system that catches new leads through a webhook. It cleans each lead’s details and logs them in Google Sheets. It then reads the service the lead wants and alerts the matching sales team in Slack: Automation, Web & Software, Training & Consulting, or Customer Experience. Leads reach the right team right away, with no manual sorting.

      Stack: n8n, Webhooks, Google Sheets, Slack


  3. Customer Support Request Process
    • A support-ticket router that takes customer requests through a webhook and logs every ticket in Google Sheets. It sorts each request by type and sends it to the right Slack team: Delivery, Product Support, Returns & Refunds, Billing, or Customer Care. Customers get faster replies, and every ticket is on record.

      Stack: n8n, Webhooks, Google Sheets, Slack


  4. PIIT Week1 Project- Personal Profile
    • A high-converting landing page that blend clean frontend code with strategic user journey. I created sections that engage visitors, capture leads, and guide them seamlessly to action.

      Stack: VScode, HTML5, CSS, Favicon, Github


  5. Visitor & Attendance Management System
    • A digital entry and exit log for a tech training hub (PIIT). Visitors sign in through an n8n form. Each visitor gets a unique Log ID and a branded confirmation email, and their visit is stored in Google Sheets. To sign out, visitors give their name and Log ID. The workflow checks these against the sheet, blocks duplicate sign-outs, and records the exit time in Lagos time. It also builds daily HTML reports for the Administration, Training and Security teams, including a list of visitors who haven’t signed out.

      Stack: n8n Forms, Google Sheets, JavaScript, Gmail, Slack
